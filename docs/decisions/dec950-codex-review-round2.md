**Verdicts**

**PRD v4 draft 2: NO-GO** — the allocator still has mathematically impossible requirements and no valid packing/failure procedure.

**Plan rev 4: GO WITH CHANGES** — Unity and slice-first remain sound; correct the slice decision matrix, preliminary measurement claims and superseded instructions before execution.

**Must change before the owner confirms**

- **PRD:** replace the invalid allocator guarantees with a terminating, deterministic placement specification and independently checked fixtures; correct rendering/compression contracts, travel and slice acceptance; reconcile CI, accessibility, distribution and content defaults.
- **Plan:** adopt those corrections explicitly, identify preliminary allocator figures as estimates, and publish one authoritative slice scope and failure decision matrix.
- Close the specific residuals in F1–F19 and F20–F37; do not record them as resolved merely because implementation is assigned to the slice.
