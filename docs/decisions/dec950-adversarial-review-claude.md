## 3. The reasoning as a whole

**SOUND WITH CORRECTIONS.** The "what not to take" list is right and well argued (agent as modeller, MCP writes as authoring path, sub-agents instead of issues). It is undermined by three things. First, the "already ours" and "no owner" checks were made against memory, not the PRD: the bespoke pieces have owners (A1), the lessons are already written (A6), and "ten checkpoints in the slice" cannot be done (A3). Second, two changes ride under the card (A1, A5), and one of them moves judgement from the owner to the reviewer, against 9.3. Third, the article is partly strawmanned: it never proposed a brief instead of design docs (its step 3 keeps them), so the missed adoption is a one-page DEC-953 brief (reading order, day plan, acceptance matrix) distinct from the repo-wide one; and "play it" is mapped to a PNG when the only player available is the owner. 9.5 schedules no owner walk of the Day-1 plinth greybox, though question 3 (place versus chart, risk 15) could be tested there before the Day 3 image path is built, inside the owner's two days.

## 4. The owner's decision

Steelman "take none now": the slice tests the stack, not the process; the plan found the week has no slack; every adoption adds a variable, a Unity process on the one GPU, or owner time (risk 3), and the retrospective will show what the engineer actually lacked. Steelman "take all before": the first Unity PR is where convention breaks (binary scenes, LFS, hand-edited generated scenes) are most likely and least reversible, and the slice engineer enters a repository whose README names the wrong contract. The evidence favours the first with one exception, because the second's strongest point is answered by a two-hour fix. Pick 1 as corrected (README fix plus a pointer-only `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md`, not a gate). Pick 5 only as link boards under `docs/`, on your own time. Take 3 as a CI artefact once the bench harness exists, with no slice-week cost. Defer 4 to after the slice and strike its Blender clause. Reject 2. Before deciding, ask the CEO to correct on DEC-950: the ownership of the four pieces against 5.3.1, 5.6.4, 7.6.4 and 8.12; the checkpoint count achievable in the slice; the "nothing delays the slice" sentence against the brief's timing; the look-verdict addition against 9.3 and row A; and to mark PR #85, "24 assets", "seven legs" and the Unity MCP licensing claims as unverified.
