## What to cut from v1

- Unavailable-avatar roster, “your cards” HUD count and portrait browsing; retain Teferi.
- Generative synthesiser and FMOD contingency; use a small licensed/original music set alongside required ambience and spatial sound.
- Basic-land signposts until printing selection and exhibit identity are explicit.
- Per-district bespoke overrides and decorative waystone infrastructure for tiny sites.
- Completing all twenty hero scenes as a first-release dependency; retain the approved roster and additive rollout.

Policy distinction: portals/waystones as unrestricted navigation, rarity plinths as metadata, random browsing and a bounded recent-history list are adequately constrained **as individual features**. Their presence does not settle F1’s whole-product classification.

## What to add before the slice starts

- Exact checkout/data hash, Unity/package versions, asset sources and a reproducible text-driven scene-generation entry point.
- Executable layout specification plus fixtures for Dominaria, hub, gold-heavy Alara, colourless-heavy New Phyrexia, two-card sites and 0–4-card planes.
- One-page rendering allocation: HDRP/URP substitutions, texture formats/residency, image treatment and transition peaks.
- Day-by-day allocation of engineer work, owner editor work and independent hardware review; identify prerequisites outside the week explicitly.
- Named release-policy decision owner; accepted visual checkpoints; measurable performance, loading, accessibility and failure criteria.

## What the slice must prove

1. **Can the engineer deliver a reproducible standalone slice within five working days, using no more than two owner editor-days, including the required URP variant and independent review?**
2. **Does sustained Dominaria traversal pass the agreed frame-time, hitch, memory and transition limits on both named Macs under cold and warm image-cache conditions?**
3. **Can all 6,271 stands satisfy spacing, chronology, site membership and safe access while the owner accepts the ground-level result as a place rather than a radial chart?**
4. **Do third-person movement, camera/card-view transitions, river/lake rendering and directional sound meet the owner’s specified visual standard in both pipelines?**
5. **Can a visitor reach and inspect any selected card—including filtered, double-faced and uncached cases—without dead ends, altered image colours, missing attribution or dependence on network availability?**
