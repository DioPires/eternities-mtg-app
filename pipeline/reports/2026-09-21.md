**Closed form differs from the card count on 33 of 45 worlds** (marked `x`). That column is what §1.3's relaxation exists for and why §2.4 ships `rowCells` rather than a formula: `round(2*pi*sin(theta) / (aspect*dphi))` summed over the rows is only approximately the card count, and the shipped grid has to be exactly it.

**Aspect** is the worst row's deviation from 4:3. It is bounded by integer `rowCells`, not by the surface law: a two-cell polar row has an aspect of pi/2. The renderer must letterbox art into the cell's own rect and may not assume 4:3 anywhere (§2.1).
